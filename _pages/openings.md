<h2 class="h5 mt-5 mb-3">International Applications</h2>

Applications from international students are welcome for both **PhD** and **MSc** programs.

International applicants are expected to apply for financial support through the **Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)** or other relevant scholarship programs. Admission will primarily be considered for applicants who can secure scholarship funding.

Research can be conducted **in English**.

Prospective applicants are encouraged to email the following materials to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) for an initial evaluation:

- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Academic transcript
- Two-page research proposal
- Publications or other research outputs, if available

PhD program applicants should have at least one paper published in an SCI-indexed journal.

Further communication will be arranged based on the applicant's academic background, research interests, and available positions. Interested applicants will normally receive a reply within one week.
