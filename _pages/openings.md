<h2 class="h5 mt-5 mb-3">International Applications</h2>

Applications from international students are welcome for the **PhD** program at Wuhan University.

International applicants are expected to apply for financial support through the **Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)** or other relevant scholarship programs. See the scholarship [page](https://admission.whu.edu.cn/ADMISSION/SCHOLARSHIPS.htm) at Wuhan University for application details. Priority will be given to applicants who can secure scholarship funding.

Research and supervision can be conducted **in English**.

#### Research Areas

Including but not limited to the following:

- Cloud Detection and Removal
- Time-Series Land Cover/Use Mapping
- Seamless Flood Mapping and Monitoring
- Multi-Source Remote Sensing and GeoAI
- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for Environmental and Disaster Monitoring
- Disaster Impact Assessment and Resilience Analysis

####  Requirements

- A Master's degree in Remote Sensing, GIS, Geography, Geomatics, Environmental Science, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- At least **one first-author paper** published, accepted, or under major revision in an SCI-indexed journal.
- Relevant research experience in remote sensing, GIS, deep learning, or GeoAI.
- Good programming skills, preferably in Python, and strong motivation for independent research.

#### Application Materials

Prospective applicants are encouraged to email the following materials to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) for an initial evaluation:

- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- A 2–3-page research proposal
- Copies or links to published papers

Applications will be evaluated based on academic background, research experience, publication record, and research proposal. Interested applicants will receive a reply within one week.
